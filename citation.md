## Citing National Archives of Norway - Evidence Explained

To cite documents from [Digitalarkivet](https://www.digitalarkivet.no/en/) (the Digital Archive of Norway), you should include the original source information, the digital repository details, and a permanent URL. 

**Core Elements of a Citation**

When citing archival material found on the platform, include the following layers: 

1. **Original Record Details**: Parish or location, type of record (e.g., *Ministerialbok*), specific volume or book number, and the entry description (name, date, and page/entry number).
2. **Digital Repository**: Identify the National Archives of Norway (*Arkivverket*) and the website Digitalarkivet.
3. **Digital Reference**: Use the **Permanent Image Link** or **Permanent ID** provided by the site rather than the browser's address bar URL, as these are designed to remain stable.
4. **Access Date**: State when you accessed the record. 

**Citation Examples**

**Church Record (Evidence Explained / Genealogy Style)**

> Voss Parish Church (Voss, Norway), "Kirkebok," 1823–1837, Deaths and Burials, p. 324, entry 93, Anna Sjursdtr; digital image, *National Archives of Norway, Digitalarkivet* (https://media.digitalarkivet.no/view/39543/58 : accessed 7 Feb 2026). 

**Census Record**

> 1865 Census for Norway, digital image, *National Archives of Norway, Digitalarkivet* (https://www.digitalarkivet.no/en/census/person/pf99000000000010 : accessed 7 Feb 2026); citing original record in the Regional State Archives. 

**Tips for Accuracy**

- **Use Permanent Links**: Always look for the "Permanent ID" or "Permanent image link" in the record's metadata to ensure your citation remains valid.
- **Identify the "Source of the Source"**: [Digitalarkivet](https://www.digitalarkivet.no/) often provides a "Complete source information" section. Include the archival reference number (e.g., *SAT, Ministerialprotokoller...*) if it is available to help other researchers find the physical original.
- **Standard Abbreviations**: The platform uses the [HISTFORM standard](https://www.digitalarkivet.no/en/content/7/help) for abbreviations in transcribed census data; keep these in mind when transcribing names exactly as they appear in the digital record.
